# Touring the Tabletop tutorial

[日本語](../tabletop/README.md) · [Guide](README.md)

The tutorial lays out explanations and real examples of cards, lines, pins, clips, stamps and other tools on one large desk.

**[Open the current tutorial — Japanese](https://zuntan.github.io/tabletop/?desk=desks/tabletop-guide.tabletop)**

It opens directly in your browser without installation or an AI connection. The desk content is Japanese; changing UI language does not translate it. A separate English tutorial is planned but not yet available.

## First steps

The central introduction appears on opening. Select its nearby green wayfinder and press the ▶ popup to follow the route. Try fitting the suggested card to the view, then continue toward “01” (Card).

You can follow numbers in order or visit whichever tool interests you. There is no need to finish the entire tour.

## Explanations and samples

The numbered inner cards introduce tools. The outer cards contain more detail, with examples farther out.

- Green wayfinders lead toward detailed explanations.
- Blue wayfinders return to the introduction for that tool.
- Pins1 and2 let you travel between a detail card and its sample using previous/next controls.

Wayfinders retain zoom while following their routes. Click the desk to stop during travel.

## Explore freely

Zoom with the wheel; pan with Space+drag or the middle mouse button. Select a card and use Fit selection to read it. Clear the selection and use Fit all for an overview.

The spatial arrangement is part of the tour. Look at distant samples and props in the margins too.
