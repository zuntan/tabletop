# Tabletop — the idea behind the desk

[日本語](../tabletop-philosophy.md) · [Guide](README.md)

Tabletop is a desk where humans and AI can spread out material, look at it, point to it and move it while thinking together.

It began with the image of a shared sketching desk: a place to leave things in progress, make changes together and pick up the next thought.

## Gather around the same desk

AI can refer to material you put down, and you can see what AI adds. A card in the upper right, the space between two shapes, the thing being pointed at: position, arrangement and overlap can join the conversation alongside the content.

“Let's put these two together.” “Keep this one a little further away.” You can continue that exchange on the same desk.

## No need to rush toward a finished product

You do not need to know the outcome before starting. Arrange shapes to think. Leave a hypothesis on hold. Spread out an interesting reference and let the conversation wander.

Thinking, discussing and trying things are uses of the desk in their own right. You may make something, or simply talk. Work in progress has a place here too.

## You do not always need to edit together

One participant can place a diagram while the other only points. Add a fresh sheet beside it or just look for a while. Humans and AI need not edit the same thing at every moment; each can take part in their own way.

## Give AI the same desk

Whether you ask for work or think something through together, the desk provides shared references. Keeping text, shapes, images, links and pins in one place can reduce the effort of preparing, saving and attaching material again.

Human and AI both bring thoughts to the table.

## Let a desk remain a desk

Tabletop avoids prescribing too much of its purpose. A circle can be part of an explanation, a game piece or something without a name yet. Stamps and cutouts can be useful in whatever way fits the moment.

Save a desk to resume later, or take its contents away as a PDF. These are tools available on the desk too.

**The desk does not decide its use. You and your AI can find that together.**
